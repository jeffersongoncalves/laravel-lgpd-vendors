## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-16

First release: LGPD vendor register.

- `Vendor`: role (processor, controller, joint controller), risk tier, DPA dates and checklist, country and art. 33 transfer mechanism, termination and data return
- `issues()`: missing, expired or incomplete DPA, missing transfer mechanism, review due by risk tier, data return pending
- `php artisan lgpd:vendors [--issues] [--json]`
